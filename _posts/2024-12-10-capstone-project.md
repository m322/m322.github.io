Today's session concluded the first part of the course, where we studied and discussed the fundamentals of AI safety, mainly from a theoretical perspective.

We covered a lot:
1. AI and the years ahead. How and why might we build future AI systems?
2. What is AI alignment? What do we need to do to ensure AI systems do what we want, and why is this difficult?
3. Reinforcement learning from human (or AI) feedback. Why do AI systems today mostly do what we want?
4. Scalable oversight. How might we scale human feedback for more powerful and complex models?
5. Robustness, unlearning and control. Can we prevent oversight methods from being gamed?
6. Mechanistic interpretability. How might we understand what’s going on inside an AI model?
7. Technical governance approaches

It is now time to dive deeper into a particular topic and work on the capstone project for the remaining weeks! I've already got a few rough ideas that need to be polished, with the help of Session 8.
